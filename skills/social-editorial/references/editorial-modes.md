# Editorial modes and acceptance

| Mode | Reader receives | Evidence and review |
|---|---|---|
| Brand introduction | What this is, whom it serves, an intelligible entry | Current offering, no invented capabilities |
| Opinion and trade-off | A supported position and a useful decision criterion | Preserve scope, examples and reasonable uncertainty |
| Work demonstration | A recognizable task, current capability and exact try link | Separate observed UI from tested outcomes |
| Maker story | A real scene, decision and consequence | Verify dates, first-person identity and causal claims |
| Creator curation | A specific recommendation and why it matters | Eligibility, attribution, own judgment, original link |
| Participation request | A useful bounded action for a suitable person | Real need, time/scope if known, voluntary participation |
| Feedback follow-through | What was noticed, changed and remains unresolved | No invented adoption, user quote or improvement |
| Cultural or future direction | Values, intended direction and its conditions | Label ambition; do not present future features as available |

Past/present/future are evidence lenses across modes, not compulsory posting slots.

Hypothetical quality contrast: describing a four-person demo repeats its listing. Discussing
how a solo visitor can find three partners adds a specific access question. That does not
prove the game has a matchmaking feature, that the maker adopted the suggestion, or that a
community will provide players. Use the angle only when the actual source supports it.

Language parity check: names, numbers, stance, eligibility, uncertainty, promises and the
intended action must survive adaptation. Fluency is not permission to strengthen a claim.

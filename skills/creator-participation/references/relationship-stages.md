# Relationship stages

| Current evidence | Appropriate next move | Stop or change condition |
|---|---|---|
| Specific fit, no previous invitation | Explain one relevant invitation if useful | No fit or no contribution: appreciate or move on |
| Invitation sent, no reply | Preserve record and wait for a real new condition | Do not chase on a timer or another channel |
| Explicit interest | Answer their question and provide one doable next step | Resolve a material missing fact rather than inventing it |
| Onboarding obstacle | Give precise help or route a reproducible issue | Do not claim a fix without verification |
| Work shared | Confirm what is actually visible and permitted | Do not infer consent for reuse, edits or promotion |
| Feedback offered | Preserve the observation and its scope | A suggestion is not acceptance or authorship transfer |
| Change actually made | Report the verified change and limitations | Do not claim causal improvement without evidence |
| Promise conditional on launch/material | Check the actual condition at the existing entry | A planned date alone does not satisfy the promise |
| Declined or private-only | Respect the boundary | Reopen only on explicit new permission or changed request |
| Unknown previous delivery | Inspect the original request/result | Never resend to find out whether it worked |

Quality test: can the person understand what they are being asked to do, why it is relevant,
and what they are not committing to, without enduring a sequence of engagement questions?

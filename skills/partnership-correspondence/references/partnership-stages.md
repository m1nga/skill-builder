# Partnership stage guide

| Stage | Useful output | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Exploratory fit | Recipient-specific contribution plus one fit question | No implied agreement or speaking commitment |
| Requested proposal | Deliverable, audience benefit, known scope and open terms | No invented availability, credentials or costs |
| Formal application | Check actual rules and permitted assistance | Preserve human-only authorship restrictions |
| Sponsor or paid programme | Accurate options and commitments for authorized decision | Do not purchase or imply a budget |
| Accepted collaboration | Confirm exact agreed responsibilities and next deliverable | Do not expand rights, scope, timing or audience access |
| Inbound question | Direct answer with needed evidence | Avoid resending a whole pitch |
| Unknown previous send | Reconcile original receipt | No alternate sender or channel to bypass uncertainty |
| Specific form/access hold | Prepare permitted material and work other cases | Do not bypass verification or treat one hold as universal |

Hypothetical example: a design group may benefit from three interface scenarios about the
difference between commenting, editing and publishing. A fit enquiry offers the available
scenarios and asks whether they suit the group's current programme; it does not advertise
an accepted workshop, measured outcomes or a confirmed facilitator date.
